## The films I carry with me

### Interstellar（星际穿越） — 10.0 / 10.0

Directed by Christopher Nolan.

> Do not go gentle into that good night; Old age should burn and rave at close of day. Rage, rage against the dying of the light.

### Stalker（潜行者） — 9.5 / 10.0

Directed by Andrei Tarkovsky.

> It is so quiet out here, it is the quietest place in the world.

### Robot Dreams（机器人之梦） — 10.0 / 10.0

Directed by Pablo Berger.

> Afraid you'll forget me. Afraid you'll always remember me.

### How to Train Your Dragon（驯龙高手） — 8.6 / 10.0

Directed by Chris Sanders and Dean DeBlois.

> We go with the sky. Our hearts belong to flight.

### Che（切·格瓦拉） — 8.5 / 10.0

Directed by Steven Soderbergh.

A film I carry less for its form than for its conviction, sacrifice, and the weight of revolutionary faith.

### Oppenheimer（奥本海默） — 9.5 / 10.0

Directed by Christopher Nolan.

> They won't fear it until they understand it. And they won't understand it until they've used it.

> When they've punished you enough, they'll serve you salmon and potato salad, make speeches, give you a medal, and pat you on the back telling you all is forgiven. Just remember, it won't be for you… it would be for them.

### Castle in the Sky（天空之城） — 8.6 / 10.0

Directed by Hayao Miyazaki.

> No matter how many weapons you have, no matter how great your technology might be, the world cannot live without love.

> Utopia is not a perfect place; it is a reminder of traversing the fantasy.
